Camera controls

Getting started

7. Camera screen

Hold the iPhone sideways. The photograph shows an iPhone 16 Pro before photo 1. Match each blue number to the descriptions below. The version number is no longer shown on the camera screen.

Figure 16. S012Cam 2.0 camera screen. The displayed values are examples.

No.ControlWhat to do
1Guide and support (?)Tap to open the S012Cam website.
2Settings (gear)Tap to open settings.
3Interval (s)Set the time between photos in Timer mode. The default is 2 seconds. Gyro mode does not use this setting, so the field is dimmed.
4ResolutionChoose the calculation resolution: Full, 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/5, 1/6, 1/8 or 1/10. Lower resolutions calculate faster.
5ExposureAdjust exposure compensation from −2 to +2 EV in 1/3 EV steps. Try this when the red or blue channel is weak.
6ShutterChoose 1/60 to 1/1000 s, or Custom. The default 1/200 s suits handheld use. If photos are repeatedly rejected as blurred, choose a faster shutter.
7ISOThe app chooses ISO. Tap the preview to meter while keeping the selected shutter. △ means ISO reached its limit. The image may be too dark or bright, or the shutter may have been lengthened.
8Camera buttonsTap to choose a camera; yellow marks the selection. This iPhone shows 5, 1× and .5 from top to bottom. Available cameras depend on the model; see section 1.
9Shutter buttonPress at 0° for photo 1. In Gyro mode, turn the dial and hold still for automatic photos 2–4. Press during a set to capture the current photo immediately.
10Saved measurementsTap to open the saved measurements list.
11Photos 2–4Choose Gyro or Timer. Gyro is the default. Turn the dial, hold still and wait for the automatic photo. Timer uses the fixed interval set at number 3.
12Dashed measurement boxKeep the subject inside. After photo 1, its outline helps align the next photos. The eye button shows or hides the outline; it is not yet visible here.
