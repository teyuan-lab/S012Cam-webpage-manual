Frequently asked questions

Is there an Android version?

S012Cam is currently available for iPhone only. Please stay tuned for an Android version.

Can S012Cam measure circular polarization?

Not currently. Measuring circular polarization requires additional optics, including a waveplate such as a quarter-wave plate. Suitable waveplates are costly, and the current S012Cam hardware accessories do not support this measurement.

S012Cam uses a rotating linear polarizer to obtain linear-polarization information. It does not measure the circular Stokes component S₃. The website’s left/right circular and arbitrary-polarization animations demonstrate the principles; they do not indicate that the app can measure circular polarization.

Why are some pixels magenta?

Magenta marks pixels the app could not measure. They may be overexposed, too dark or outside the area where the four photos align. Try adjusting the exposure or resting the phone on a stable surface.

Why are the corners dark?

A phone case blocks the upper-left corner of the Ultra Wide view; remove it for the full view or use Wide or Tele. For other dark corners, lift the rotator off and snap it on again so the camera window is centred over the rear lenses.

Why do the angles look wrong?

Check the order of the four positions and repeat the first-use reflection check to confirm the polarizer axis.

What does Photos Don’t Line Up mean?

The app could not align enough of the measurement box across all four photos. The percentage describes that capture; magenta shows areas that do not line up. Moving subjects, shadows and near/far parallax can cause it. Correct the cause and choose Retake. Continue without aligning may leave false polarization at displaced edges.
