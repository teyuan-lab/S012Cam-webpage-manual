Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Summary

S012Cam does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Everything the app captures and calculates stays on your iPhone.

What the app accesses

PermissionWhy
CameraTo take the four RAW photos used to calculate polarization. Photos are only taken during a measurement that you start with the shutter button. In Gyro mode, photos 2–4 of that measurement are taken automatically when you turn the polarizer.
Motion sensorsDuring a measurement, the gyroscope tells the app when the polarizer has been turned and when the iPhone is steady again. These readings, and a small log of them kept with the measurement, stay on your iPhone. No permission is needed for this, and nothing is sent anywhere.
Photo libraryOnly if you choose to save or open images there. S012Cam does not read your photo library on its own.

Where your data is stored

Photos, measurements and exported files are stored only in S012Cam's storage on your iPhone. They leave your device only when you choose to share or export them yourself, for example with AirDrop, the Files app or email. Deleting a measurement in the app, or deleting the app, removes this data from the device.

If your iPhone's backup is turned on, iOS may include the app's data in your own iCloud or computer backup, under your Apple account. S012Cam's developers have no access to it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No collection of location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data.
  • No data is sent to any server.

Children

S012Cam does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
