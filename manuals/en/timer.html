Timer mode

Capture and calculation

Timer: reach each angle before the interval ends

A guided sequence. Play it or select a step. This illustrates the workflow, not a live app screen; Gyro has no fixed capture interval.

Press the shutter for photo 1

Reach 45° before the interval ends for photo 2

Reach 90° before the interval ends for photo 3

Reach 135° before the interval ends for photo 4

Set Photos 2–4 (11) to Timer. Set Interval (3), 2 seconds by default. Press the shutter for photo 1. Turn the dial to each prompted angle before the next interval ends. Photos 2–4 use the fixed interval. In Gyro mode, Interval is dimmed because it is not used.
