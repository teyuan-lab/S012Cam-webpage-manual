S012Cam

Polarization camera for iPhone · iOS 15.6 or later

Make the polarization of light visible.

A normal photograph records brightness and colour. S012Cam adds the direction and degree of linear polarization. Explore reflections from glass, scattered skylight, LCD light and polarization effects in transparent materials. Use it for teaching, hands-on experiments and polarization imaging.

The app calculates a scene’s linear-polarization maps from RAW (DNG) images taken through a linear polarizer at four angles. The Polarizer Rotator provides indexed angles for numerical reference. A hand-held polarizer with estimated angles is useful for viewing patterns, but is unsuitable for quantitative reference.

From capture to analysis.

Assisted four-angle capture

Gyro mode senses turns and continues capture automatically. Timer offers fixed-interval capture. Outline guidance, blur checks and position checks help obtain a usable set.

Capture control

Shutter priority, exposure compensation, calculation resolution and available-camera selection help adapt capture and processing to the scene.

Save and export

Measurements stay on your iPhone. Keep the original DNGs, share PNGs or export 32-bit floating-point TIFF maps for further analysis.

Compatibility

Works on iPhones with iOS 15.6 or later; developed and tested on iPhone 16 Pro. Available cameras depend on the model. Older iPhones may take longer to calculate a measurement.
