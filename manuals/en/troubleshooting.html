12. Troubleshooting

What you seeWhat to do
Camera PermissionOpen Settings and allow camera access for S012Cam.
An expected camera is absentButtons depend on the iPhone model. Wait for startup calibration. Use an available camera.
Stays at “Turn to XX”, not “Hold still”Turn to the requested angle, lightly touch or gently tap the phone once, then hold still without changing the framing.
No automatic photo in Gyro modeTurn to the prompted angle, then hold still and match the outline. After about 8 seconds, use Capture now, Redo previous or Cancel. The shutter also captures immediately.
Plain area messagePosition cannot be checked in a textureless area. Keep the phone steady or frame a textured subject.
Repeated blur rejectionUse a faster shutter and support the iPhone. Keep the current dial angle while the app retries.
Next photo comes too soon in Timer modeIncrease Interval. Follow the angle prompt before each photo.
ISO warning or weak signalCheck ISO (7) and Tap for details. Adjust Exposure (5) or Shutter (6) before retaking a full set.
Coloured fringes or alignment failureHold steadier, use a tripod, step back or choose a flatter scene. Try Align and Calculate.
Angles consistently wrongCheck the 0° axis and the order 0°, 45°, 90°, 135°. Repeat the polarizer check in section 6.
A dial stop repelsPush the round magnet out through its rear hole and flip it.
Dial does not click or scrapesCheck all 12 round magnets are flush. Re-seat the bearing and remove support material or dirt.
Dark cornersRe-centre the camera window. Remove the case for the full ultra wide view.
Rotator slipsCheck the magnet sticker orientation in section 6.
Plate or polarizer comes looseRinse dusty gel pads with water and air-dry them completely.
