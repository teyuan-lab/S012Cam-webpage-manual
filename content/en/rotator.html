The kit contains a base, dial, MagSafe plate, MagSafe magnet sticker, thin ball bearing, 12 round magnets, 3 cube magnets, a linear polarizer and 9 nano gel pads. Have small pliers, a utility knife, a soft cloth, a fine marker and a blunt pin or toothpick ready.

Keep small, strong magnets away from children, pets, pacemakers and other medical implants. When trimming supports with a utility knife, cut away from your hands.

  1. Check the printed partsIf your print used supports, remove them with small pliers and carefully trim any residue. If it was printed without supports, skip removal. Check that the magnet pockets and bearing seat are clear.
  2. Insert the round magnetsKeep the magnets stacked and take each one from the same end. Always put the same face in first. Press 8 into the base and 4 into the dial until flush.
  3. Seat the bearingTurn the dial upside down. Place the bearing in the recess and press evenly on its outer ring until it meets the lip.
  4. Fit the dial to the baseLower the dial and bearing so the bearing’s inner ring slides over the raised hub.
  5. Insert the cube magnetsPlace the 3 cube magnets in their seats through the centre of the dial. They hold the bearing’s inner ring against the base.
  6. Test the dialTurn it once in the direction of the arrows. It should click every 45°, eight times per turn, without scraping. If a stop repels the dial, push the reversed magnet out through the hole behind it and flip it.

Fit the polarizer and mount the rotator

Leave the protective film on. Look through the polarizer at glare from a flat, horizontal surface. Turn it until the glare is darkest and draw a vertical line on the film to mark the transmission axis. Turn the dial to position 1 and fit four pads. Align the mark with the ↔ arrows, press the polarizer onto the pads and remove the protective film.

The rotator holds on the iPhone by MagSafe. With both blue films still on, hold the magnet sticker against the iPhone to find the side that holds firmly. Peel the film from that side and press it into the ring groove of the MagSafe plate, with the tail in the slot. Press one gel pad into each of the five square pockets on the plate, peel the second blue film, and press the plate onto the back of the base for 10 seconds.

Snap the rotator onto the back of the iPhone with the camera window over the lenses, and check each lens in the app. It also works with a case, but the case blocks the upper-left corner of the Ultra Wide view.

Before the first measurement, hold the phone level and measure glare on a flat, horizontal surface. The result should show horizontal polarization. If it shows vertical, refit the polarizer after turning it 90°.
